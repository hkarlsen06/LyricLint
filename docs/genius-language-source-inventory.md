The page is community-maintained and mutable. Some entries retain English headers, vary by genre, distinguish original songs from translations, contain uncertain translations, or provide terms without a clear semantic mapping.

## Known policy exceptions requiring explicit modeling

- Japanese, Thai, Indonesian, and Dutch sources direct users to English section headers.
- Korean distinguishes original Korean songs from Korean translations.
- Czech and Slovak prefer English headers, with local alternatives in some contexts.
- Finnish permits English headers and may prefer them.
- German choices vary between rap and other genres.

These must be encoded as language-pack policy, not flattened into a single translation dictionary.
